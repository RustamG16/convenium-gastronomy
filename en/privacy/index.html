Hosting and server logs

This website is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit, GitHub processes technically necessary data – IP address, date and time, the page requested, browser and system details – to deliver the page and protect it from abuse. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure and stable delivery (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; transfers to the USA rely on the European Commission’s adequacy decision of 10 July 2023. How long GitHub keeps these logs is set by GitHub; we have no access to them ourselves.

Your inquiry by email

The inquiry form sends nothing through this website. It only prepares a text you can copy. When you write to us, we process your details to answer the inquiry and, where relevant, prepare a proposal (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We delete the correspondence once it is no longer needed; if a contract follows, we keep it for the statutory period (usually seven years, § 132 Austrian Federal Fiscal Code).

No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser’s local storage and uses no analytics or advertising services. Fonts, images, videos and scripts are loaded from the same server; no third parties are embedded. The link to the concept website opens a separate site.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Contact us at the email address above. You may also lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority: Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, www.dsb.gv.at.
